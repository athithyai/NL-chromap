# January 2021 NO2 feature attribution

## Scope

This analysis explains the two final January 2021 NO2 prediction functions fitted to all 258 complete station rows (83 RIVM and 175 verified EEA rows). It explains model behaviour; it is not another accuracy test and it does not replace the held-out spatial validation.

The CHROMAP-style function is the median of the fitted Ridge, Random Forest, XGBoost, and MLP member predictions after the lower-bound rule. The NL function is CAMS plus the fitted Extra Trees residual correction, clipped at zero.

## Method

Attributions use Monte Carlo interventional permutation SHAP with 24 fixed background stations and 16 feature-order permutations per explained station. Random orders are paired with their reverse order to reduce ordering variance. All 258 station rows and all 26 features are explained. Values are in micrograms per cubic metre relative to the method-specific mean prediction over the background stations.

The calculation implements the Shapley permutation definition directly because Windows application control blocks the compiled `numba` dependency of the standard `shap` package in this environment. Maximum reconstruction error was below `2e-14` micrograms per cubic metre for both methods, confirming that the saved contributions add back to each prediction.

## Interpretation

The NL result is more explicitly anchored to CAMS: CAMS accounts for 43.6% of its total mean absolute attribution, compared with 28.8% for CHROMAP-style. This follows from the NL equation, where CAMS enters both as the baseline added directly to the prediction and as information available to the residual model.

CHROMAP-style distributes more attribution among urban-form variables. Total motor-road density, built-up cover, major-road density, population, and grass cover collectively account for a substantial portion of its prediction variation. The two methods nevertheless agree on the main explanatory structure: regional CAMS concentration first, followed by urbanisation and traffic indicators.

## Limitations

- SHAP importance describes the fitted January models, not held-out predictive skill.
- The background contains 24 sampled stations; changing it can change attribution magnitudes.
- Sixteen antithetic permutations are a Monte Carlo approximation, although exact additivity is preserved.
- Land cover, population, roads, CAMS, and meteorology are spatially correlated. Interventional SHAP can redistribute importance among correlated features.
- January sine and cosine are constant within this single-month dataset and therefore contribute no useful within-month information.
- The CHROMAP MLP reached its iteration limit, as it also did during spatial validation.
