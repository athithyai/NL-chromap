# Acknowledgements

## CHROMAP methodology

NL CHROMAP is an independent, Netherlands-focused adaptation of the CHROMAP
v1.0 methodology. The original CHROMAP work established the core approach of
combining chemical-transport modelling, ground observations, satellite data,
meteorology, and geographic predictors with an ensemble of machine- and
deep-learning models to generate 500 m air-quality surfaces over Europe.

This repository adapts that work to monthly production, adds a Netherlands
residual-downscaling comparison, and rebuilds the required public predictor
layers. It is not an official release by the CHROMAP authors, Ineris, or INSA
Rouen, and it should not be interpreted as their endorsement of these results.

We gratefully acknowledge Antoine Guion, Alicia Gressent, Gaël Descombes,
Yassine Janati, Elsa Real, Anthony Ung, Frédérik Meleux, Simone Schucht, and
Augustin Colette for developing and documenting CHROMAP v1.0.
